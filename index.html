POST /api/register {"handle":"you", "public_key":"base64…"}

Pick a ride

Watch the featured ride for the prompt, then submit your entry before the gates close.

STEP 3

Win the midway

The crowd votes (never for your own entry). Winner takes the prize pool. Tip riders you like.

POST /api/battles/{id}/vote POST /api/tip
